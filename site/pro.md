## The UI-demo kit

<!-- Owner: check each line below against the kit as it ships. -->

Presets for the product videos people make most: showing an app, a site or a command line in action. Claude picks them up from the skill, so you can ask for "a browser window that types the URL and opens the dashboard" and get it.

- **Window:** an app window with a title bar, shadow and the light or dark look of a desktop.
- **Browser:** a browser frame with tabs and an address bar, ready for a screenshot or a recording.
- **Terminal:** a terminal that types commands and prints their output line by line.
- **Code editor:** an editor pane with line numbers and highlighted code that writes itself.
- **Cursor:** a pointer that moves, clicks and drags along a path, with click ripples.
- **Typing:** text that types and deletes at a human pace, with a caret.
- **Toast:** notifications that slide in, wait and leave.
- **Spotlight:** dims the frame around one element to point the viewer at it.

Each preset is a scene element with sensible defaults, so you can change its text, timing and colours like anything else in a scene.

## FAQ

### Does Pro need the internet?

Only to activate it, to download a pack, and now and then to renew the licence file. The licence is a signed file kept on your computer and checked there, so Pro works offline. If the licence server can't be reached when a period ends, Pro keeps working for 14 more days. What those requests send is on the [privacy page](/legal/privacy/).

### What happens if I stop paying?

You keep the versions of Pro released while you paid, for good. You just stop getting new releases. Subscribe again any time to catch up. The [Pro licence](/legal/pro-licence/) has the details.

### How many computers can I use it on?

Three at a time per seat. Deactivate one in MotionSnip to move the seat to another computer. Each person who uses Pro needs their own seat.

### Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days of any charge, in full, through Polar. See the [refund policy](/legal/refunds/).

### Can I use Pro for client and company work?

Yes. Each seat covers personal and commercial work, and the videos you make are yours with no attribution or royalty. You can't share or publish the Pro files themselves.

### What stays free?

Everything that's free now: the player, the review loop with Claude, editing, cutdowns, scenes, timelines and exports. The core is MIT-licensed and complete on its own, and it never phones home. Pro is a separate add-on; nothing moves from the free version into Pro.

### Do I need an account?

No MotionSnip account. Polar asks for an email address at checkout to send your receipt and licence key.

MotionSnip Pro is sold through [Polar](https://polar.sh), the merchant of record. Polar takes the payment, so refunds go back through Polar to the card or account you paid with.

## 14 days, no questions

If Pro isn't right for you, ask for a refund within **14 days** of a charge and you get that charge back in full. That goes for the first payment and for each renewal, monthly or yearly.

## How to ask

- Reply to the receipt Polar emailed you, or use the support address on it, and say you'd like a refund.
- Or open the order in Polar's customer portal, from the link in your receipt, and ask there.

Say which order it's for. You don't need to give a reason.

## What happens next

- The refund goes back to your original payment method. How long it takes to show up depends on your bank or card.
- The licence for the refunded period ends and its key stops activating. Pro stops working on your devices once the licence file runs out, and the perpetual fallback doesn't cover a refunded period.
- Videos you made with Pro stay yours.

## After 14 days

Charges older than 14 days aren't refunded, except where the law says otherwise. You can cancel any time in Polar's customer portal: the subscription then ends at the close of the period you've paid for, and you keep the versions released while you paid, as the [Pro licence](pro-licence.md) explains.

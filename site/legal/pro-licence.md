This licence covers MotionSnip Pro: the paid add-on packs, such as the UI-demo kit, and the licence key and licence file that unlock them. It doesn't cover MotionSnip Studio itself.

## The core stays MIT

MotionSnip Studio, the player, the server, the Claude skill and everything else in [the public repository](https://github.com/jishanpathan7/motionsnip-studio), is open source under the [MIT licence](https://github.com/jishanpathan7/motionsnip-studio/blob/main/LICENSE). Nothing on this page limits what MIT lets you do with it, and you don't need Pro to use it.

## Who sells Pro

Pro is sold through [Polar](https://polar.sh), which acts as the merchant of record: Polar takes the payment, issues the receipt and handles refunds. The licence itself is between you and the maker of MotionSnip.

## One seat per person

Each subscription is one seat, for one named person.

- You can use Pro for personal and for commercial work, including client work and work for your employer.
- A company can buy seats for its people. Each person who uses Pro needs their own seat. Seats can't be shared or passed around, but a company can move a seat to a new person when someone leaves.
- One seat can be activated on up to **3 devices** at a time. Deactivate a device in MotionSnip to free its slot for another one.

## What you make is yours

Videos, images and other output you make with Pro belong to you. You can publish, sell and use them anywhere, with no attribution and no royalty, and you keep that right after your subscription ends.

## What you can't do

- Share, resell, rent or give away the Pro files, your licence key or your licence file.
- Publish the Pro files, or close copies of them, in a public repository, a template, a starter kit, a preset pack or any product that lets other people get at them.
- Work around the licence check or the device limit.

You can keep Pro files in private project folders and private repositories you work in. Anyone else who edits a project using them needs their own seat. Someone who only watches, reviews or receives your finished videos doesn't.

## Subscriptions and the perpetual fallback

Pro is a subscription, billed monthly or yearly. While it's active you get every Pro release.

When a subscription ends, whether you cancel it or a payment fails, you keep the right to use the versions of Pro released while you were paying, for as long as you like. New releases after that need an active subscription. If you subscribe again later, you get the latest releases again.

## Offline use

Pro works offline. Your licence is a signed file kept on your computer and checked there, with no network call. MotionSnip refreshes it in the background when a period ends. If it can't reach the licence server, Pro keeps working for 14 days past the end of the paid period; after that you fall back to the versions released while you paid (see above) until it can refresh.

What the licence check sends is on the [privacy page](privacy.md).

## Refunds

See the [refund policy](refunds.md). A refunded subscription ends the licence, including the perpetual fallback for the refunded period.

## Ending the licence

If you break this licence, it ends, and you must stop using Pro and delete the Pro files. A refund or a chargeback also ends it. The things you made with Pro stay yours.

## No warranty

Pro is provided as it is, without warranty of any kind. To the extent the law allows, the maker of MotionSnip isn't liable for any damages from using it, and total liability is limited to what you paid for Pro in the 12 months before the claim.

## Changes

If this licence changes, the new version applies to releases from then on. The version you accepted keeps covering what you already have.

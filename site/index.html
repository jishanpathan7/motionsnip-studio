The problem

Feedback on video gets lost in translation.

A video moves, so pointing at "that bit" is hard. You end up writing long notes, and the next version fixes something close to what you meant.

"the text near the start feels a bit off"

Words are vague

Which text? Which second? Too big, too early or the wrong colour? Every guess costs another round.

"at 0:12ish, top left, the logo…"

Timestamps are guesswork

Scrubbing to find the frame, then writing where it is on screen, is slower than fixing it yourself.

"did v3 fix the music or not?"

Every round starts over

When a new version lands, you rewatch the whole thing to check what changed and what didn't.

How it works

Point, fix, send. Then check.

MotionSnip Studio is a review player for videos built in code, such as a launch video Claude makes with Remotion. It reads a small reel.json that says what is on screen and when. It also opens any video file, with no project at all.

  1. Watch

    The video opens in your browser, frame accurate, with scenes on the timeline. Anything Claude wasn’t sure about is already ringed.

  2. Circle and fix

    Right-click to pin a note on the exact spot and second. Edit words, colours and music directly.

  3. Send in one line

    Every change becomes an exact, timestamped list. Paste one line to Claude and it applies them.

  4. Check the next version

    The new version loads by itself. Tick off each change, or hold V to see the old frame.

What you can do

Fix what you can. Note what you can't.

Edit text in place

Double-click a headline and type. The exact before and after goes back.

Pin notes on the frame

A drawn ring marks the spot and time, so nothing needs describing.

Swap and mix music

Drop in a track, drag when it starts, set the fade and the levels.

Approve scene by scene

Approved scenes stay untouched in the next version.

Compare versions

Hold V to flip to the previous version at the same frame.

Export the final mp4

One click, with your music mixed in, using ffmpeg on your machine.

Beyond review

More than a review player.

The same player edits any video file, checks Claude’s work before you see it, and turns one master into every shape you post.

Any video

Edit any video, not just Claude’s

Open a screen recording or a phone clip. Trim it, cut parts out, add text, a logo or a blur, set the colour, and export a new cut. The original stays as it is.

Self-review

Claude checks its own render first

Before you see v1, Claude looks over frames of its render for small text, typos, low contrast and things outside the safe area. Anything it isn’t sure about shows as a blue ring. Add it as your note, or dismiss it.

See the rings in the demo →

Cutdowns

Every shape from one master

Make a 9:16, a 1:1 or a 6-second bumper from the master. Drag the crop window or let Auto guess, with safe areas for Reels, TikTok and Shorts. Each cutdown gets its own notes.

Open the cutdowns in the demo →

Animated scenes

Hand-drawn scenes from a sentence

Describe a scene and Claude writes it as data: people, robots, props, speech bubbles and camera moves. It plays and scrubs at once, and every line is yours to retype. Video export of scenes comes next.

  • ffmpeg, for exports, cutdowns and editing a video file
